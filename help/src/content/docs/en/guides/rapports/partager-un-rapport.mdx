A shared report stays **live**: each reader opens it in **My reports › Shared with me** and sees
it recomputed on the current data, **with their own access rights**. To pass on a frozen
version, share a PDF from the history.

## Share with people

1. Open the report and click **Share**.
2. In **Person to add**, type the person's **Login (uid)** (or **E-mail address**, depending on
   the instance), choose **Can view** or **Can edit**, then **Add**.
3. Click **Save**. **Copy link** gives the address of the report, to send by e-mail: Druid does
   not notify the person.

- **Can view**: the person reads the report and generates its PDF. They can **Duplicate** it to
  make their own version.
- **Can edit**: the person can also edit the report. Only the owner shares or deletes it.

## Make a report visible to everyone

In **Share**, tick **Visible to every user of this instance**. The report shows up in the
**Visible to everyone** tab of **My reports**, read-only.

## Publish an instance template

*Super administrators only.* Tick **Publish as a template of the instance**: the report is
offered to everyone in **New report**, under **Templates of the institution**, on the structure
and period they choose. Blocks, texts and filters are copied; blocks set to a given structure
keep it.

## PDF history

**History** lists the PDFs generated from the report: date, author, period and number of
publications.

- **PDF** (download): on the instances that archive PDFs, the **last 20** are kept, for **two
  years** at most.
- **shared with readers** (checkbox, owner): the readers of the report can download **this
  frozen PDF**, with the figures as you generated them.
- **Restore** (people allowed to edit): brings back the blocks, texts and scope of that version,
  with its period fixed. Confirm with **Replace the current content**.

## What happens next

Shares take effect immediately. Removing a person (**Remove**, then **Save**) closes their
access to the report, but not to the PDFs they already downloaded.

## Known limitations

- **Each reader sees their own data**: a block about a structure they cannot see shows « No
  data available… or no access to it »; nothing leaks. Only a shared frozen PDF shows figures
  computed with your rights: share that way only what the readers may see.
- **A report that names people** (researcher ranking, co-authorship network) is flagged when
  you open the sharing window: share it with care.
- **No public link**: only people with an account on the instance can open a report. For a
  public chart, see
  [Embed a chart in a website](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/integrer-une-dataviz/).
- **Without PDF archiving** on your instance, the history keeps the date, author and scope of
  each generation, without the file.
