A person can only carry one uid in Druid. Two situations still need your attention:

- **two records for the same person**, under two different uids (for example an `ext_…`
  record created from the lab website and the record coming from the LDAP directory): they
  must be **merged**;
- **a person with several memberships** (two laboratories, or a change of laboratory): you
  must say which one is the **primary** one.

Both cases arrive as **tasks** in **To process › Tasks**. Use the type filter to find them.

## Merge two records

The nightly detection creates a **Directory — two records for the same person: merge** task
when two records share an identifier (ORCID, IdRef, IdHAL, Scopus, staff number) and carry the
same name.

1. Open the task, then click **Merge** (followed by both uids). The **Merge two records**
   assistant opens.
2. Check which record is **Kept**: Druid suggests the one with a laboratory, then the
   validated one, then the most complete. Click the other record to swap.
3. Go through the **Fields to decide**: for each field that differs, Druid pre-selects a value
   and says why (most recent validation, most recent LDAP update, union of both values, fills
   an empty field…). Change it if needed.
4. Click **Merge**.

To chain several merges, click **Merge in series** at the top of the Tasks tab: the assistant
opens on each pair in turn (“pair 3 of 37”). Every merge is still reviewed and confirmed;
**Skip** leaves a pair as it is and opens the next one.

:::caution
If the two records do not have the same uid, Druid asks you to confirm that they really are
the same person. When in doubt, do not merge: open both records and check. The
**Directory — shared identifiers: same person or namesakes?** tasks are handled one by one
for this reason.
:::

## Choose the primary membership

When a person belongs to several structures, their record shows all their memberships in
the **Affiliations & history** card. Only one is **primary** (purple dot): it is the one that counts in
the lists, the dashboard and the export to SoVisu+.

- To change the primary membership, click the grey dot of the membership you want, then save
  the record.
- The other memberships become **secondary** while ongoing, **former** once their end date
  is past. Druid works it out when the record is saved.

When you create a record for a uid that already exists in another laboratory, Druid adds a
membership to the existing person: the existing membership stays primary, the new one becomes
secondary (or former if it has already ended).

## Qualify memberships left pending

A few cases cannot be settled automatically: for example a membership restored after a merge,
or a membership outside your scope. The person then shows as several records, and adding a
membership is refused. The nightly detection creates a **Directory — affiliations to qualify**
task.

1. Open the task.
2. Choose the **Main row**: the one of the person's main laboratory.
3. Choose the type:
   - **Concurrent**: the person belongs to both laboratories at the same time. The other rows
     become **secondary**;
   - **Successive**: the person changed laboratory. The other rows become **former**; you can
     give the end date of the former affiliation;
   - **To review**: undetermined case, set aside.
4. Confirm: the task is done and the person has a single record again.

If both rows carry the same laboratory, it is usually the same membership entered twice:
click **Open the merge assistant** instead.

## Restore a merge

Every merge is **logged**. The **To process › Merges** tab lists the merges carried out, most
recent first; the **Restore** button recreates the absorbed record and puts the kept record
back in its state before the merge.

## What happens next

- A merge changes the directory immediately: the absorbed record disappears from the lists,
  and a trace of the merge is added to the kept record.
- A qualification deletes nothing: it states, for each membership, whether it is primary,
  secondary or former.
- Towards CRISalid, a person is exported only once, with their primary membership.

## Known limitations

- **Only records sharing an identifier are matched**: two records of the same person with no
  common identifier (for example an external person entered twice under two spellings) are
  not spotted.
- **Restoring a merge puts the kept record back in its state before the merge**: changes made
  to that record in the meantime are lost.
- **Tasks are created by the nightly detection**: a situation that appears during the day is
  only reported the next day (or after **Run detection**).
