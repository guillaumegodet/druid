The **People** section lists all the records you can see. Search and filters let you find a
person or build a list (a lab, a status, the records to check).

## Find a person

Type in the search box at the top of the list. The search covers the **name**, the **main
laboratory** and the **e-mail**. Click a row to open the record.

## Filter the list

The drop-down lists combine several values (multiple selection):

| Filter | What it keeps |
|---|---|
| **Internal staff** (button) | Shortcut: people who are **present** and **employed by the institution**. |
| **Presence** | Present, Leaving (end announced), Left. |
| **Employer** | **Home institution**, **Other employers**, **Not specified**, or a specific employer (CNRS, Inserm…). |
| **LDAP account** (More filters) | Active account, account closing, no account. |
| **Validation** | **Validated**, **Validated (expired)** (more than 18 months) or **Not validated** records. |
| **Lab (primary affil.)** | The laboratory of the primary affiliation. |
| **Grade / rank**, **Employment type** | From the Employment card of the record. |
| **Cluster** | The cluster or faculty of affiliation. |

Below:

- **Identifiers**: click **ORCID iD**, **HAL**, **IdRef** or **Scopus** to keep only the
  records that **have** this identifier;
- **Dates**: keeps only the people whose employment period overlaps the chosen interval.

The number of records kept is shown next to the title.

## Switch to the charts view

The **Charts** button replaces the table with charts calculated on the filtered list:
breakdown by presence and employer, gender balance, age brackets, grades, employers, employment types,
breakdown by lab, HDR rate and **identifier coverage**. **List** goes back to the table.

## Act on a selection

Tick records in the list to show:

- **Group**: add the ticked records to a group of researchers (see
  [Create a group of researchers](/en/guides/structures-groupes/creer-un-groupe/));
- **Merge**: merge exactly two records of the same person (see
  [Handle a duplicate](/en/guides/personnes/traiter-un-doublon/)).

## What happens next

Nothing is changed: search and filters only change the display. Exports (**Export**) apply
to the filtered list (see [Export data and reports](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/exporter/)).

## Known limitations

- **The search does not cover identifiers** (ORCID, IdRef…), despite what the search box
  suggests: to find a person by identifier, go through their name.
- **The Identifiers filters only find the records that have the identifier.** To spot
  missing identifiers, use the
  [identifier alignment](/en/guides/identifiants/aligner-les-identifiants/) page or the charts
  view (identifier coverage).
- **The Lab filter uses the primary affiliation**: a person with a secondary affiliation to a
  lab does not appear in this filter.
- **The Dates filter** ignores records without an employment date, which remain displayed.
