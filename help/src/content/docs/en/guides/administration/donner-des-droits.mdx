In Druid, a user's rights come from the **groups** their account belongs to in the
institution's authentication service (Keycloak). The **Access rights** page shows these
groups and their members; rights are **granted** in the Keycloak administration console.

## Choose the right access

| Need | Right to grant | Where |
|---|---|---|
| See one's own laboratory | None: it is the default right of every logged-in account, derived from its record. | — |
| Follow one or several laboratories without depending on one's record (management, administration) | **Dashboard viewer** | Under the laboratory's group |
| See the whole institution (central services) | **Dashboard viewer** | At the institution level |
| Administer Druid (groups, duplicates, tasks) | **Administrator** | At the institution level |
| Manage media sources | Communication officer (media group) | At the institution level |

The same account can combine several rights. See
[Instances and features](/en/donnees/instances-fonctionnalites/#profiles-and-rights) for what
each profile allows.

## Check current rights

1. Open **Administration › Access rights**.
2. The tree follows the institution's groups: for each laboratory, its sub-groups
   (**Own-laboratory viewer**, **Dashboard viewer**, **Administrator**…) and their **Users**.
3. Groups marked **interpreted by Druid** give a right in Druid; the others (**other app**)
   concern other CRISalid applications (SoVisu+, Projects).

The page is **read only**.

## Grant a right

1. In the Keycloak administration console (the institution's realm), find the user.
2. Add them to the group you want: **under the laboratory's group** for a right limited to
   the lab, **directly under the institution** for a right on the whole institution.
3. The user logs out and back in to Druid for the right to take effect.

If you do not have access to the Keycloak console, pass the request on to the technical
team with the person's username, the laboratory and the right needed.

## What happens next

At the next login, Druid recalculates the person's rights: the corresponding sections and
data appear.

## Known limitations

- **A “lab” right placed at the wrong level** (in a container group rather than under the
  lab) is not recognised: check in Access rights that the person does appear under the right
  laboratory.
- **The default right depends on the record**: a person without a record, or whose record
  mentions a former laboratory, does not see the right lab. Correct the record rather than
  adding a right.
- **On instances without Keycloak**, access is managed through a list of authorised
  addresses kept by the technical team: Access rights does not exist there.
