The **Sustainable Development Goals** view (**Steering** family) shows how the publications of
the structure spread over the **17 SDGs** adopted by the United Nations in 2015 (health, clean
energy, climate, life below water…). It serves sustainability and social responsibility reports,
institutional files and labs that want to place their themes against these goals.

## Where the goals of a publication come from

Druid classifies nothing itself: it reads the goals **OpenAlex** assigns to each publication.
OpenAlex uses the classifier of the **Aurora** university network, a language model trained to
recognise, in the title and abstract, the work related to each goal. For each publication it keeps
the goals whose **score** exceeds its threshold; a publication may carry several goals, or none.

What to keep in mind:

- **About half of a corpus carries a goal.** Fundamental, theoretical or methodological work, and
  a large part of the humanities, get none: the « without SDG » share is not missing data.
- **Only the publications known to OpenAlex are tagged.** A record that comes from HAL alone,
  without a DOI, has no goal.
- **SDG 3 (health) dominates** in a university with a teaching hospital: the **Excluding SDG 3**
  box lets the sixteen other goals be read.
- **Tags change**: OpenAlex re-tags the publications when its classifier changes. The figures of
  two exports far apart in time may differ.

## The charts

| Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Key figures** | Publications with at least one goal (and their share of the corpus), goals covered out of 17, main goal, mean number of goals per tagged publication. |
| **Publications by Sustainable Development Goal** | One bar per goal present, in the official order and colours. The tooltip gives the share of the tagged publications and the share of the corpus. A publication counts in **each** of its goals: the bars add up to more than the number of tagged publications. |
| **Yearly evolution by goal** | Stacked areas of the six goals with the most publications over the period, the others folded into « Other SDGs ». |
| **Share of publications tagged with a goal, by year** | The proportion of each year's publications carrying at least one goal. A flat curve supports year-to-year comparisons; a step signals a change of classifier or of sources. |
| **Goals by lab or by team** | On a composite structure, the goals of each member lab; otherwise those of each team. **Share (%)** compares profiles, **Publications** compares volumes. |
| **Open access and impact by goal** | For each goal: publications, open access share, mean FWCI, top 10 % share and international share, with the rules of the [Impact view](/en/donnees/indicateurs/#impact). |

A click on a bar, an area or a count opens the **publication list** filtered on that goal (and
on the year, lab or team clicked). The four charts are available in
[My reports](/en/guides/rapports/ajouter-un-graphique/) and can be
[embedded](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/integrer-une-dataviz/) in a web page.

## In the publication list

The **Sustainable Development Goal** filter of the [list](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/liste-des-publications/)
offers each goal present in the corpus, **With a goal** and **Without goal**. The CSV export adds
a `sdgs` column with, for each publication, the goal numbers and the classifier score.

## Known limits

- **A guidance indicator, not an audit**: the classifier recognises topics close to the goals, it
  does not judge the contribution of a work to sustainable development. SDG 16 (peace, justice,
  institutions) and SDG 4 (education) come out on many humanities publications whose link with
  the goal is remote.
- **Not comparable with rankings**: the *THE Impact Rankings* and SciVal rely on Elsevier's
  keyword queries, not on OpenAlex; the volumes do not match.
- **Compare labs of the same field**: a humanities lab and a health lab do not have the same share
  of tagged publications; always read the « without SDG » share next to the figures.
- The view stays **hidden** while the export of the structure carries no goal: regenerating the
  dashboard from the [ETL console](/en/guides/administration/console-etl/) is enough.
