A **group** brings together researchers who do not necessarily belong to the same
structure: a scientific council, the leaders of a project, a cohort of new recruits. It has
its own **bibliometric dashboard**.

## Create the group

**From the staff list** (the quickest way for many members):

1. In **People**, filter and tick the people.
2. Click **Group**.
3. Choose an existing group or type the name of a new group, then **Validate**.

**From the Groups section**:

1. Open **Groups** and click **New**.
2. Give the group a name.
3. **Add a member**: search for people by name.

A new group is only kept once it has at least one member.

## Make members “harvestable”

A group's dashboard is built from its members' **identifiers**, not from an affiliation:
each member must have an identified **ORCID** or **OpenAlex author**. The group's
**Bibliometric dashboard** section shows how many members are harvestable.

For a member without an ORCID, click **Identify the author**: Druid looks for the matching
OpenAlex author profiles (in their laboratory if possible); choose the right one using the
number of publications and the name variants.

## Generate the dashboard

1. In the group's record, choose the **Period**.
2. Click **Generate the dashboard**. Harvesting takes a few minutes; its progress is shown.
3. Click **View the dashboard**. The group also appears in the dashboard's **Structure or
   group** selector.

After adding members or identifiers, click **Regenerate**.

## What happens next

- Group membership is stored on each member's record.
- The group's dashboard works like a structure's (see
  [Choose a structure, a period, a scope](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/structure-periode-perimetre/)).

## Known limitations

- **Members without an ORCID or an OpenAlex author are left out** of the group's corpus.
- **A fragmented OpenAlex profile** only brings back part of the publications: identify all
  the person's profiles (see [Researcher identifiers](/en/donnees/identifiants-chercheurs/)).
- **One generation at a time**: if another dashboard is being calculated, yours waits.
- **The dashboard does not update itself**: regenerate it after each change in membership.
