The **My institution** tab gathers what concerns the Druid of your institution: its plan, its accounts and how
your colleagues get in.

## Plan and accounts

The top band shows the **plan** (Trial, Team, Complete), the **accounts** used out of those allowed and the depth of
the **publication history**. The trial includes one account, yours; the Team and Complete plans add more. An invited
account that has not signed in yet already counts.

## Invite a colleague

1. In **Members**, enter their e-mail address and choose their **role**:
   - **Owner**: everything, including the accounts and the plan;
   - **Administrator**: edits the directory, runs the alignments and the dashboard builds;
   - **Reader**: reads everything, edits nothing;
   - **Lab account**: reads and edits its labs only (enter their acronyms).
2. Click **Invite**. Your colleague receives an e-mail that opens the login page of your Druid, address already
   filled in: they ask for a sign-in link and are in.

Until they sign in, the row shows « Invited, never signed in » and **Resend** sends the invitation again. The role
menu changes the role of a member; the bin removes them and closes their sessions at once. The last owner of a Druid
can be neither demoted nor removed.

## Access requests

Enter the **mail domains** of the institution (for instance `univ-example.fr`). A colleague with an address of these
domains, without an account, who asks for a link on the login page sends you an **access request**: you receive an
e-mail and the request shows at the top of the tab. **Invite as reader** opens an account for them (you can change
the role afterwards), **Dismiss** files the request.

The login page always answers the same, account or not: it does not reveal who has an account.
