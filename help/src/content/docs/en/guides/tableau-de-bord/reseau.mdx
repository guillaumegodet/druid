The **Network** tab draws who publishes with whom. Each circle is a researcher (its size
follows their number of publications), each line one or more co-signed publications. The
graph reads on the period chosen at the top of the dashboard.

## The lab's network

By default, the graph shows the lab's researchers and their co-authorships, coloured by
team. The **Min. publications** slider hides the least active authors to keep the graph
readable. Drag the circles, zoom with the mouse wheel; hover over a researcher to bring out
their co-authors.

## Open the network to other labs

1. In the banner above the graph, click a suggested lab (**+ IETR**…): the suggestions are
   the labs your lab co-publishes with most over the period. The **Other lab…** field lets
   you search for another one.
2. The graph then shows the researchers of your lab **and** of the added labs,
   **coloured by lab**. Lines between two different labs are emphasized, lines inside a lab
   fade.
3. Tick **Cross-lab links only** to keep only the bridges: the researchers who co-signed
   with another lab.
4. Tick **Show the other labs as grouped nodes** to see all the university's labs at a
   glance: every lab not selected becomes a diamond, linked to the researchers of your lab
   who published with it.
5. **Click a line** to list the common publications, with their DOI link.

**Outlined** researchers belong to several labs: they are often the ones making the link.
The selection is kept in the page address: copy it to share the view.

## Who sees what

- With a right on **the whole institution**, every researcher of the chosen labs is visible.
- With a right limited to **your lab**, researchers of other labs only appear when they
  co-published with your lab, and only the publications co-signed by your lab are listed.

## Known limitations

- **The inter-lab mode relies on the university corpus**: it may differ slightly from the
  lab-only network (different update dates, abbreviated name spellings).
- **About a quarter of the signatures are linked to no lab** (abbreviated names in OpenAlex,
  people missing from the staff lists): these authors are not shown. The rate is given below
  the banner.
- **A researcher is identified by their name**: two namesakes from different labs may be
  merged.
- **Large collaborations** (more than 50 authors) are excluded by default, since a single
  publication links dozens of people there; untick the box to include them.
- **Beyond 400 researchers**, the least active are hidden: raise the threshold or narrow the
  selection.
- The inter-lab mode is not available on every instance.
