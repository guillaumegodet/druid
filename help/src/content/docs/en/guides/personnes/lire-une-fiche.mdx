The record brings together everything Druid knows about a person in a structure. It is where
you check a piece of information, correct it and validate the record.

## Open a record

1. Open **People**.
2. Find the person with the search or the filters (see
   [Search and filter staff](/en/guides/personnes/rechercher-filtrer/)).
3. Click their row. **Back to the list** takes you back to the list.

## The header

- **Name, photo and status** (Internal, Leaving, Left, External), with the validation badge
  if the record has been validated: **Validated**, **Expired** or **Validated — conflict**.
  Hovering over the badge shows the source and date of the validation.
- **UID**: the person's identifier in the institution's directory (or `ext_…` for an
  external person), and the date of the last synchronisation.
- The action buttons: **Validate the record** (or **Validated record**), **Report a
  correction** (administrators), **Export PDF** and **Save**.

Depending on the record and your instance, the header also offers **Directory** (the
person's page in the institution's online directory) and **Grist** (the shared spreadsheet
holding all the records, for those who have access to it). The **?** button opens this help
page.

## The cards

| Card | Content |
|---|---|
| **Researcher identifiers** | IdRef, ORCID, IdHAL and its number, Scopus, OpenAlex profiles. Each identifier links to the profile. |
| **Personal details** | Title, name, name in use, first name, date of birth, nationality, UID. |
| **Employment & contract** | Employing institution, grade / rank, employment type, employment start and end dates, HDR and year obtained. |
| **Cluster, faculty, doctoral school, site** | The person's administrative affiliations. |
| **Affiliations & history** | The person's laboratories and teams, with the primary affiliation, the type and the membership dates. |
| **Media presence** | The declared public accounts and profiles (CV, social networks, blog) and the latest mentions of the laboratory in the media. |

## Edit a record

1. Change the fields you want directly in the cards.
2. For employment and membership dates, you can enter a year, a month or a full date (see
   [Enter imprecise employment dates](/en/guides/personnes/dates-imprecises/)).
3. Click **Save** at the top of the record.

The photo is changed with the pencil on the photo (**Edit photo (URL)**): enter the address
of a public image (lab page, directory, HAL). Without a photo, Druid shows the initials.

## What happens next

Changes are written **immediately** to the shared directory. They are passed on to CRISalid
at the next export (if your instance allows it), and appear in the dashboards at their next
regeneration.

## Known limitations

- **LDAP directory data take precedence on display** for title, date of birth and grade: a
  correction made in Druid on these fields can be hidden by the directory's value. For an
  error of this kind, see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
- **A person can have several records** (one per laboratory). The record shows all their
  memberships, but validation and saving apply to the record that is open.
- **The status cannot be edited directly**: it is calculated. To correct it, validate the
  record or fill in the end dates (see
  [Correct a person's status or affiliation](/en/guides/personnes/fiabiliser-statut-rattachement/)).
