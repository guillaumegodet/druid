A lab has a dozen identifiers and no registry links them all: the **RNSR** for the French open
science monitor and scanR, the **ROR** for collaborations, the **HAL structure** and **HAL
collection** for deposits, the **OpenAlex institution** for the corpus, the **IdRef** for ABES and
SoVisu+, **Wikidata** for the rest. The **Structures** tab of the alignment page brings them
together: **one row per lab or team, one column per identifier**.

## What the search does

1. **Bridges first.** From every identifier already confirmed, Druid reads the record and derives the
   others: the HAL record of a lab often carries its RNSR, ROR and IdRef; the ROR record carries the
   Wikidata item; OpenAlex knows the ROR. A value reached by two independent paths, or whose record
   carries the acronym or the unit number, is a **strong candidate**.
2. **Then a search**, for the types still empty: by name, acronym, unit number (“UMR 6004”), city and
   supervising institutions. Every candidate gets a score; a university proposed for a lab, or a
   closed record, is penalized.
3. **Checking** (“Check existing ones”) reads the records of the confirmed identifiers again: a record
   that disappeared, closed or contradicts another identifier becomes a **conflict**.

## Steps

### 1. Run a search

1. Open **Alignment tools › Researcher identifier alignment** and click **Structures** at the top
   right.
2. Click **Find missing identifiers**. The search only takes the structures never searched; **Search
   this structure again** in the detail of a row forces one structure.
3. On an instance without server jobs (Cloudflare), the candidates are computed by an external job:
   the tab is then used to review them.

### 2. Read the badges

- **strong candidate**: one sure value. Tick the box to accept it.
- **N candidates ?**: several strong values, for instance two HAL records (old and current). Open the
  row and choose; several HAL or OpenAlex records may be confirmed together.
- **N to review**: medium candidates, to read before deciding.
- **conflict**: the confirmed identifier is contradicted by a bridge, or its record disappeared.
- **confirmed**: nothing to do. **not found**: searched, nothing convincing. **·**: never searched.

### 3. Decide

1. **Check all strong candidates** then **Confirm** validates in bulk; that is the bulk of the volume.
2. In the detail of a cell, every candidate shows its record (link), acronym, unit number, city,
   supervising institutions, works count (OpenAlex) and the bridges that reached it. **Refuse**
   discards a candidate with a note: it will never be suggested again.
3. **Type** a value by hand (identifier or URL) when you know it.
4. The confirmed values are the ones the exports (SoVisu+) and the bibliometric dashboard read; for
   the multi-valued types, the **primary** value is the one harvested.

## On the structure record

The **Identifiers & links** tab of the record lists the confirmed identifiers, their source (typed,
imported, alignment), the date of the last check and a link to each record. You can add, remove or
change the primary value there; the candidates are decided on the alignment page.
