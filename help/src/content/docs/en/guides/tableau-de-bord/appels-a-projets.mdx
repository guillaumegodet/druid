The **Calls for proposals** view (**Steering** family) gathers the open or announced calls of the
programmes the institution follows and brings them together with the researchers of the
structure, from their publications. It does not replace the monitoring of the research office or
of the Europe office: it points out where the work of a team *appears* close to a call, and who
may apply, as a basis for discussion.

## Where the calls come from

| Source | Programmes | Update |
|---|---|---|
| **Funding & Tenders portal** of the European Commission | Horizon Europe (clusters), ERC, MSCA, EIC, Missions, Infrastructures, Widening… | every night |
| **ANR website** | generic call (AAPG, one call per research axis), specific calls, France 2030 calls operated by the ANR | every week |
| **Added by hand** | Region, foundations, labex, other France 2030 operators, internal calls, ResearchConnect export… | on demand |

The collected programmes are chosen in **Administration › Calls for proposals**, which is also
where a call no collector reads is added (see
[Manage calls for proposals](/en/guides/administration/appels-a-projets/)). A call added by hand
only appears here once **validated**.

## Where the matches come from

For **thematic calls** (a Horizon Europe cluster, a mission, a specific ANR call, a regional
call…), an automatic process (the CRISalid *Topic-to-Researcher Match* batch) reads the text of
each call: a language model writes queries, looks for the closest publications in the CRISalid
graph of the institution, checks the candidates and writes a **justification**. Druid imports the
result and crosses it with its directory: only researchers known to Druid are shown, with a link
to their record.

Calls **open to any subject** (ERC, Marie Skłodowska-Curie actions, EIC Accelerator, AAPG axes…)
have no theme to match: they get no thematic match. For those reserved to a career stage, the
**Candidates** view lists the eligible people (see below).

The banner at the top of the view says **which structures** the computation covered. When the
structure you are looking at is outside that perimeter, a warning says so: its researchers only
appear as co-authors of publications of the perimeter, which says nothing about its own coverage.

## The three views

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Calls** | One call per row: title, identifier, programme, next deadline, number of researchers retained (including the **strong** matches). Unfold the row to read the summary, the call, the dates, open the official page (*Funding & Tenders* portal, ANR website or page of the call) and see the researchers. |
| **Researchers** | One block per researcher, with the calls where they come up. This is the view to show a researcher: « here is where your work shows up ». |
| **Candidates** | Institution right only: for a call reserved to a career stage, the researchers who are eligible or to check. |

The filters apply to the **Calls** and **Researchers** views:

- **free search** on the title, identifier and classification of the call;
- **funder** (category: Europe, ANR, Regional, Private / foundations, International…), then
  **programme**;
- **subset** of the programme, named after the chosen programme: Horizon Europe cluster, mission,
  scheme (ERC, MSCA), AAPG domain…;
- **Include closed calls**: by default, only calls with a deadline ahead are listed;
- **Calls with researchers only** (Calls view, ticked by default): hides the calls without any
  researcher retained. Calls **added by hand** stay shown even before the automatic process has
  run on their programme.

## Read a match

Each match card carries:

- a **strength** assessed by the model from the publications: **strong**, **plausible** or
  **weak**;
- a **relative score bar**: the best candidate of the call is at 100 %, the others in proportion.
  It ranks researchers *within a call* and cannot be compared across calls: a candidate at 100 %
  may be rated weak;
- the **justification** written by the model (translated into French when the interface is);
- the **publications** that led to the match (the first three, then « N more publications »), with
  the DOI link when Druid knows it, otherwise a search on OpenAlex.

## Give your opinion

Under each match, three buttons: **Relevant**, **Not relevant**, **Already applying**. One opinion
per person and per match, the last one replaces the previous; the count of opinions is shown next
to each button. These opinions are used to assess and improve the automatic process (they can be
exported for the CRISalid team): give some even on obvious matches.

## Spot eligible candidates

The **Candidates** view is about calls reserved to a career stage, such as the ERC *Starting* and
*Consolidator* grants. Druid reads the rule **in the text of the call** (for instance « successful
defence of PhD between 1 January 2017 and 31 December 2026 »): it changes from one edition to the
next and is never hard-coded. Choose the call; the view recalls the rule, the sentence of the text
it comes from and the deadlines, then lists the people of the structure.

The **PhD year** comes from [theses.fr](https://theses.fr), read through the **IdRef** of the
record (checked again every six months, or as soon as the IdRef changes): without an IdRef, or for
a thesis missing from theses.fr (foreign, old thesis), the person does not appear. The thesis found
is also shown on the record, under the HDR line.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Eligible** | PhD defended within the window of the call |
| **To check** | date known to the year only and falling on a bound of the window, or PhD just before the window: eligible only with a documented extension (maternity or parental leave, illness, national service, clinical training) |

Filter by verdict and grade, and export the list as **CSV** for the Europe office or the research
office. Only the PhD date is checked: the other conditions of the call (previous grants, host
institution, time spent in Europe…) remain to be checked with the person.

## Who sees what

- With a right on **the whole institution**, the matches of every structure and the
  **Candidates** view.
- With a right limited to **your lab**, the matches of its researchers only; no Candidates view,
  which crosses careers.
- Every signed-in user can give an opinion; exporting the opinions is reserved to institution
  rights.

## Known limits

- **Only the publications known to the CRISalid graph**: a researcher whose publications are not
  there, or not vectorised yet, shows up on no call. The perimeter banner says which structures
  the computation covered.
- **One computation per programme, at a given date**: the matches are not recomputed when the
  directory or the publications change, and a call added since the last computation has no
  researchers yet.
- **The dates come from the source** at collection time: check them on the official page of the
  call before committing.
- **The AAPG axes** are kept after stage 1 closes: they describe the profile of a project until
  the next edition.
- The Candidates view only reads rules based on the **PhD date** for now (ERC).
