The **Structures** section is the reference list of laboratories, teams, faculties and
institutions. Each record describes a structure according to the common model (see
[Structures](/en/donnees/structures/)).

## Find a structure

1. Open **Structures**.
2. Search by name, acronym or RNSR, or filter by **level**, **status**, **cluster** or
   **supervising body**.
3. The **Charts** button shows the **Structure hierarchy** (inclusions and participations)
   as a chart; **List** goes back to the table.
4. Click a row to open the record.

## Update the record

1. Go through the tabs: **Identification**, **Identifiers & links**, **Missions & topics**,
   **Memberships**, **Lifecycle and lineage**.
2. Change the fields you want.
3. To attach a team to its laboratory, or a laboratory to its supervising bodies, use the
   **Memberships** tab:
   - **Inclusions**: the parent structure (team → laboratory, laboratory → cluster);
   - **Participations**: supervising bodies and other links. A structure is chosen from the
     database, or added through its **UAI code** or its **ROR identifier**. Mark the **Main
     supervision**.
4. For a merger, a split or a change of name, add a link in **Lifecycle and lineage** (**+ Add
   a link**) and update the status.
5. Click **Save**.

The **Dashboard** button opens the structure's bibliometric indicators, if they exist.

## Create a structure

**New structure** opens a blank record. Choose the level and the type, fill in the name and
the acronym, then **Create**. The local identifier is assigned on creation.

## Synchronise

The list's **Synchronise** menu offers, depending on your instance:

- **Force Grist refresh**: rereads the structures from the shared directory;
- **Import from LDAP**: compares the structures with the LDAP directory's reference system
  and suggests creations and updates.

Structures are sent to CRISalid from **Administration › Synchronise with SoVisu+**, together
with the researchers.

## What happens next

Changes are written to the shared directory. The list's hierarchy is recalculated from the
inclusions. CRISalid receives the structures at the next synchronisation.

## Known limitations

- **The parent laboratory and the cluster are read only** in the Identification tab: they are
  derived from the inclusions (Memberships tab).
- **The local identifier cannot be changed** after creation: it is what links the structure
  to people and to CRISalid.
- **Deleting and merging structures** is not available from the list: go through
  [support](/en/guides/support/).
- **Creating a structure in Druid does not create its dashboard**: see
  [Run the ETL console](/en/guides/administration/console-etl/).
