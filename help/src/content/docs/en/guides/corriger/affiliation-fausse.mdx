An affiliation can be wrong in two places: **in the directory** (a person's laboratory) or
**in a publication** (the institution a bibliographic database linked an author to). They
are corrected differently.

## In the directory: a person's laboratory

A person's laboratory or team is wrong on their record: correct the **Affiliations &
history** card of the record and save. See
[Read a researcher record](/en/guides/personnes/lire-une-fiche/) and
[Memberships and affiliation](/en/donnees/appartenances-rattachement/).

## In a publication

### Spot suspicious affiliations

For each structure, Druid can compare the **raw affiliation** written by the authors (as
OpenAlex receives it) with a list of expected and forbidden keywords. Publications whose
affiliation does not match are **suspicious**.

1. In **Administration › ETL console**, open the structure (technical administrators).
2. Fill in the structure's affiliation keywords and save.
3. In **OpenAlex affiliation checks**, click **Detect suspicious affiliations**.

Suspicious ones are stored with the status **Détectée** (detected). Druid administrators
find them in **People › To process › OpenAlex affiliations** (read only, with the **To
process only** filter).

### Handle a suspicious affiliation

1. Open the publication (link to OpenAlex) and compare the raw affiliation with the
   institution assigned.
2. If the error is confirmed, report it to OpenAlex, using the correction procedure described
   in the OpenAlex help centre ([help.openalex.org](https://help.openalex.org)), with the
   link to the publication and the correct affiliation.
3. In the ETL console, set the row to the **Soumise** (submitted) status and note the date
   and your remarks, then **Save to Grist**.
4. When OpenAlex has corrected it, set it to **Traitée** (done). If it was not an error, set
   it to **Ignorée** (ignored).

The **Process in the ETL console** button, from the OpenAlex affiliations tab, opens the
right structure directly.

### In HAL

If the error is in a HAL deposit (wrong research structure chosen when depositing), the
depositor or the laboratory's HAL contact edits the deposit.

## What happens next

- A correction in the directory is immediate in Druid, and visible in the dashboards at
  their next regeneration.
- A correction at OpenAlex usually takes several weeks. It appears in the dashboard at the
  following regeneration.

## Known limitations

- **Detection depends on the keywords**: too broad, they let errors through; too strict,
  they flag correct affiliations that are worded differently.
- **Druid does not correct OpenAlex**: it helps spot and follow up errors, the correction is
  made at OpenAlex.
- **An affiliation badly written by the author** (without the structure's name) is not an
  OpenAlex error: the solution is to apply the signature charter in future publications,
  and to deposit the older ones in HAL with the right affiliation.
