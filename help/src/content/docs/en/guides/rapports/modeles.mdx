A template creates a complete report in two clicks: you choose the structure, the period, the
scope and, depending on the template, one or two parameters. The resulting report is then **a
report like any other**: add, remove or move blocks as you like.

All templates **leave out the charts that do not apply** to the structure (no teams, no
strategic axes, no charter scores…) and the sections left empty.

## Structure report

**For**: a complete review of a laboratory, a faculty or the institution, for instance for an
evaluation report.

**Contents**: one section per visible dashboard tab (overview, collaborations, impact and
citations, books, journals, APC, funding, strategic axes, signature charter, teams, PhD
students, researchers, network, sources), with its charts, and key figures at the top of the
overview and impact sections.

This is the template offered by the **PDF report** button of the dashboard. The report is long:
hide or delete the sections you do not need.

## International collaborations

**Parameter**: a partner **Country**, or **All partner countries**.

**Contents**:
- overview: share and evolution of international co-publications, types of collaboration;
- partner countries: map, main countries, evolution, European Union share;
- partner institutions: main partners, flows, network;
- themes and impact of the **international co-publications** only (a filter of those blocks).

## Collaboration with a university or group

**Parameter**: one or several **partner institutions**, or a predefined group (EUniWell for
instance). Druid suggests adding the **affiliated institutions** (hospitals, institutes) that
also co-signed (**Add them**).

**Contents**: an introductory paragraph, an AI summary, the key figures of the collaboration
and of its impact, the overview, journals, people and labs involved, a breakdown by university
(from two partners), an AI analysis by major theme, the other partners of these co-publications
and, as an annex, the list of co-publications.

**Methodological choices**, recalled in the report:
- publications with **more than 50 authors** (large consortia) count in the volumes but are
  **left out of the impact indicators**;
- impact is compared with a **comparable reference**: the international co-publications of the
  structure in the same subfields, weighted like the collaboration;
- the footer reads « Internal working document ».

## Funding analysis

**Parameter**: a category of **Funders** (ANR, Europe…), or **All funders**.

**Contents**: a note on funding data, an AI summary, the key figures (funded publications and
share of the corpus, distinct funders, ANR, Europe, impact of funded articles), the main
funders, categories, evolution, funding by lab, then the themes, impact and partners of the
**funded publications**.

**Good to know**:
- funding is what the publication **acknowledges** (OpenAlex funders, ANR and European projects
  from HAL); only part of the publications acknowledge one;
- the impact of funded articles is compared with that of the structure's **articles** without a
  funder (chapters and conference papers, often without FWCI, would distort the comparison);
- categories are assigned from the funder names, on a best-effort basis.

## Journals and publishing policy

**Parameter**: a **Publisher**, or **All publishers**.

**Contents**: an AI summary, the key figures (distinct journals, Q1 share, open access, access
at NU and national licences, APC, signature charter compliance), the main journals and
quartiles, open access and journal access, article processing charges (APC) and the signature
charter.

Sections without data for the structure (journal access, charter) are not created.

## Empty report

A report without blocks, on the chosen structure, period and scope: add charts, key figures and
texts yourself, or fill it from the dashboard with **Add to a report**.

## Instance templates

Under **Templates of the institution**, **New report** also offers the reports published as
templates by the super administrators of your instance (see
[Share a report](/en/guides/rapports/partager-un-rapport/#publish-an-instance-template)).

## Known limitations

- **A template is not a live link**: changing the template, or Druid, does not change the
  reports already created.
- **AI summaries are empty at creation**: generate them in the editor (see
  [Write the texts with AI](/en/guides/rapports/textes-ia/)).
- **Without the structure's data** (dashboard not generated), the report lists every chart of
  the template, including those that will be unavailable.
