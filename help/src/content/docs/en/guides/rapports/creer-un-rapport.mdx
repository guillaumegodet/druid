A report brings together charts, key figures, texts and a list of publications about a
structure and a period. Druid **saves** it: you find it again in **My reports**, edit it, share
it and generate its PDF as often as you need. The figures are **recomputed every time the
report is opened**, from the dashboard data.

## Steps

1. Open **My reports** in the menu, then **New report**. From a dashboard, the **PDF report**
   button opens the same window, already set to the structure, period and scope shown.
2. Choose a **Template**: **Structure report**, **International collaborations**,
   **Collaboration with a university or group**, **Funding analysis**, **Journals and
   publishing policy**, or **Empty report** to start from scratch (see
   [Report templates](/en/guides/rapports/modeles/)).
3. Set the **Structure**, the **Period** and the **Scope**, then the parameters of the template
   (partner institutions, country, funder category, publisher).
   - **Fixed years**: for instance 2021 to 2025, for a review;
   - **Last years**: for instance the last 5 years, possibly **incl. current year**. The period
     moves forward by itself from one year to the next.
4. Give the report a **Name** and click **Create**. The report opens in the editor.
5. Read the preview, on the right. In the left-hand column, for each block:
   - **Move up** / **Move down** to change the order;
   - **Hide from the PDF** to keep it without printing it;
   - **Delete**;
   - a click on the block opens its settings (title, note printed under the chart,
     parameters).
6. Add blocks with the buttons at the top of the left-hand column:

   | Button | What it adds |
   |---|---|
   | **Chart** | A dashboard chart, picked from a searchable list. |
   | **Key figures** | A row of indicators: overview, impact, collaboration, funding, journals. |
   | **Section** | A heading that starts a new part of the report. |
   | **Text** | A free paragraph (headings, lists, bold, links). |
   | **Publication list** | A table of the report's publications, printed as an annex. |
   | **AI text** | A summary or an analysis by theme written by AI (see [Write the texts with AI](/en/guides/rapports/textes-ia/)). |

7. If needed, fill in the **Description (printed on the cover page)** and the **Footer note**
   (for instance « Internal working document »).
8. Click **Generate the PDF**. Druid renders the charts, then downloads the file.

## Filters and scope of a block

The report filters (a partner, a country, a funder category…) are shown as chips next to the
period; the cross of a chip removes the filter. They apply to every block.

A block can have **its own scope**: another structure, another period or extra filters. This is
the case of a chart added from the dashboard while keeping what it showed (see
[Add a chart to a report](/en/guides/rapports/ajouter-un-graphique/)), or of the impact blocks
of a template, which leave out large collaborations. The block settings say so (« This chart
has its own scope »); **Use the report scope** removes it.

## What happens next

- The report is **saved automatically**: the indicator at the top goes from « Unsaved
  changes… » to « Saved ».
- Every generated PDF is recorded in the report **History** and, on the instances that allow
  it, **archived** (see [Share a report](/en/guides/rapports/partager-un-rapport/#pdf-history)).
- Only you see the report until you share it.

## Known limitations

- **A greyed-out chart in the preview** makes no sense for the chosen structure (« Not
  available for… »): no strategic axes, no identified teams, no charter scores… It is not
  printed. The chart list flags these cases before you add one.
- **« This chart says little under the current filters »**: for instance the breakdown by
  funder category when the report is already restricted to ANR.
- **A chart may be missing from the PDF** if it could not be captured: Druid says so after the
  generation. Generate again.
- **The figures change with the data**: a report opened again after a dashboard regeneration
  shows the new figures. To keep a frozen version, keep the PDF (or its archive in the
  history).
- **On a read-only instance** (demo), reports are kept in your browser only: no sharing and no
  history.
- **If two people edit the same report**, the second one sees « Changed elsewhere » and must
  **Reload the saved version** before going on.
