The record brings together everything Druid knows about a person in a structure. It is where
you check a piece of information, correct it and validate the record.

## Open a record

1. Open **People**.
2. Find the person with the search or the filters (see
   [Search and filter staff](/en/guides/personnes/rechercher-filtrer/)).
3. Click their row. **Back to the list** takes you back to the list.

## The header

- **Name, photo and presence** (Present, Leaving, Left), the **LDAP** tag if the person has an
  account in the institution's directory (“closing” if it is about to close), and the
  validation badge if the record has been validated: **Validated**, **Expired** or **Validated — conflict**.
  Hovering over the badge shows the source and date of the validation.
- **UID**: the person's identifier in the institution's directory (or `ext_…` for an
  external person), and the date of the last synchronisation.
- The action buttons: **Validate the record** (or **Validated record**), **Report a
  correction** (administrators), **Export PDF** and **Save**.

Depending on the record and your instance, the header also offers **Directory** (the
person's page in the institution's online directory) and **Grist** (the shared spreadsheet
holding all the records, for those who have access to it). The **?** button opens this help
page.

## The cards

| Card | Content |
|---|---|
| **Researcher identifiers** | IdRef, ORCID, IdHAL and its number, Scopus, OpenAlex profiles. Each identifier links to the profile. |
| **Personal details** | Title, name, name in use, first name, date of birth, nationality, UID. |
| **Employment & contract** | Employing institution, grade / rank, employment type, employment start and end dates, **FTE (working time)** and **Research FTE** (0 to 1; empty = not provided, 0 = no research time — used by the [publication rate per FTE](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/chercheurs-effectifs/)), HDR and year obtained; below, the defended **PhD thesis** read on [theses.fr](https://theses.fr) through the IdRef of the record (year, institution, discipline). |
| **Cluster, faculty, doctoral school, site** | The person's administrative affiliations. |
| **Affiliations & history** | The person's laboratories and teams, with the primary affiliation, the type and the membership dates. |
| **Digital identity** | The declared public accounts and profiles (CV, social networks, blog). The media mentions are read in the dashboard (Watch). |

## Edit a record

1. Change the fields you want directly in the cards.
2. For employment and membership dates, you can enter a year, a month or a full date (see
   [Enter imprecise employment dates](/en/guides/personnes/dates-imprecises/)).
3. Click **Save** at the top of the record.

### Where the civil status and employment values come from

On instances on PostgreSQL, the date of birth, the grade, the title, the employment type, the employer and the
employment dates keep the value of **each source**: LDAP directory,
HR file, partner directory, lab website, lab file. The record shows the value of the most trusted
source, and a label next to the field name says which one (**LDAP**, **HR file**, **Lab website**…).

- **A value typed in the record is a correction**: labelled **Corrected**, it wins over every
  source, the LDAP included, and a later import no longer overwrites it.
- **If a source changes its value after the correction**, the **To review** label appears and the
  record is listed in [To process › Source conflicts](/en/guides/personnes/taches-a-traiter/#source-conflicts).
  Nothing changes until an administrator chooses.
- Saving the record again without touching the field does not create a correction.
- **Click the label** to see what each source says (value, date). On a corrected value, **Back to the sources** removes
  the correction: the most trusted source takes over again.
- If the date of birth or the grade changed while you had the record open (LDAP sync, another manager), the updated
  value is kept; if you changed it too, saving is refused: reload the record, then correct it again.
- After **merging two records**, the values of the absorbed record stay among the sources of the kept one; a
  correction it carried shows as the **Merged record** source, and among the source conflicts when it contradicts a
  correction of the kept record.

The photo is changed with the pencil on the photo (**Edit photo (URL)**): enter the address
of a public image (lab page, directory, HAL). Without a photo, Druid shows the initials.

## What happens next

Changes are written **immediately** to the shared directory. They are passed on to CRISalid
at the next export (if your instance allows it), and appear in the dashboards at their next
regeneration.

## Known limitations

- **On an instance on Grist, LDAP directory data take precedence on display** for the title, the
  date of birth, the grade and the employment type: a correction made in Druid on these fields can
  be hidden by the directory's value. For an
  error of this kind, see [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/).
- **A person can have several records** (one per laboratory). The record shows all their
  memberships, but validation and saving apply to the record that is open.
- **The presence cannot be edited directly**: it is calculated. To correct it, validate the
  record or fill in the end dates (see
  [Correct a person's status or affiliation](/en/guides/personnes/fiabiliser-statut-rattachement/)).
