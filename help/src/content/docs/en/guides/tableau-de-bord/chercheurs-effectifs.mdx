The **Researchers** tab crosses the list of the lab's validated members with its
publications: headcount, research FTE, publication rate per FTE and per age bracket, age
pyramid. It answers application questions such as « how many publications per permanent
research FTE and per year, by age, over 2022-2025? ».

These charts only appear once the structure's member list has been regenerated since
October 2026 (ETL console). They are not offered for groups nor in publicly shared dataviz.

## Choose the population

The banner at the top of the tab sets who is counted. It applies to every chart of the tab,
including the ranking of the most prolific researchers.

- **Presets**: **Everyone**, **Statutory members and PhD students** (without associate
  members) or **Permanent staff**.
- **Membership**: statutory, associate, secondary, visiting member, or not provided. It is
  the membership type entered in the « Memberships » card of the record.
- **Category**: permanent staff (tenured, permanent contracts), non-permanent staff
  (fixed-term contracts, ATER, post-docs, associates), PhD students, emeriti. It is derived
  from the grade of the record, otherwise from the employment type.
- **Presence**: **Current members**, or **Present during the period**, which adds the
  validated members who left during the period, counted pro rata of their presence.
- **Period**: specific to these indicators, by default the last four complete years. The
  current year is not fully harvested yet: if you include it, a warning reminds you that its
  rate is underestimated.

The settings are kept in the page address: **Copy link** shares the view as it is.

## Read the indicators and charts

- **Indicators**: headcount, research FTE (and the part estimated from the grade),
  publications per research FTE per year, share of the members who published over the
  period.
- **Publication rate by age bracket**: publications ÷ (research FTE × years of presence),
  by bracket (under 35, 35-44, 45-54, 55 and over). The age is the person's age **in the
  year of the publication**. The **All brackets** line gives the rate of the whole
  population.
- **Publications by age bracket and year**: the same, year by year.
- **Age pyramid**: headcount or research FTE by bracket and category, age in the current
  year.
- **Publications per member**: how many members have 0, 1-2, 3-5… publications over the
  period.

Click a bar, a table cell or the rate indicator to open the list of the matching
publications.

## Export for an application

The **Rate by age bracket** table repeats the figures of the chart: people, research
FTE-years, publications and rate. **Download CSV** exports it with the structure, the period,
the chosen population and the method note. Each chart can also be exported as an image (see
[Export data and reports](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/exporter/)).

## Method

- **Research FTE**: the value entered in the record (« Employment & contract » card).
  Otherwise, a default from the grade, flagged « estimated »: teacher-researcher 0.5,
  researcher 1, support staff 0. A research FTE of 0 entered in the record is kept.
- **PhD students and emeriti never count in the rate**, even with a research FTE, nor do the
  members without research FTE. They stay in the headcount and the pyramid.
- **Presence**: each year only counts for the share spent in the lab, from the membership
  dates of the record. Without dates, the person counts over the whole period.
- **Whole counting**: a publication counts once in each bracket where it has an author
  present that year. The **All brackets** line counts it only once: it is therefore often
  below most bars, this is not an error.
- **Statistical secrecy**: a bracket of fewer than 3 people is merged with a neighbour (for
  instance « 45 and over »); a too small group of unknown age is not shown.

## Known limitations

- **Authors are recognised by their name**: a member publishing under another spelling
  counts zero publications. The number of members without a matched author is shown.
- **Only validated members count**, and former members who are no longer in the lab's
  directory are not taken into account.
- **Age and FTE depend on the record**: a missing or wrong birth date, grade or research FTE
  skews the rate. Complete the records before an application (see
  [Read a researcher record](/en/guides/personnes/lire-une-fiche/)).
