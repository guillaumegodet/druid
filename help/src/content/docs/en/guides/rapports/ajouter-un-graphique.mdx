While browsing the dashboard, you spot a chart that would fit a report in progress. You can send
it there directly, without going back to the report editor.

## Steps

1. In the dashboard, set the **structure**, the **period**, the **scope** and, if needed, the
   filters (partners, country…) of the chart.
2. In the chart toolbar, click **Add to a report**.
3. Choose the report: one of yours, a report shared with you for editing, or **New report**
   (then type its **Report name**).
4. If the report covers another structure, another period or other filters, Druid says so
   (« The report covers… ») and asks you:
   - **Follow the report scope**: the chart is recomputed on the report structure, years and
     filters;
   - **Keep what the dashboard shows**: the chart keeps its own scope, for instance to compare
     two laboratories in the same report.
5. Click **Add**, then **Open the report** or **Back to the dashboard**.

## What happens next

The chart is added **at the end of the report**. In the editor, move it with **Move up** and
**Move down**, or put it under a section. Like every block, it is recomputed each time the
report is opened.

## Known limitations

- **Only the charts of the report catalogue** have the button: those that depend on a selection
  made during the session (Benchmark peer group, for instance) do not have it yet.
- **A report holds at most 150 blocks**: beyond that, Druid refuses the addition.
- **A report shared with you read-only** is not in the list: duplicate it first to make your
  own version.
