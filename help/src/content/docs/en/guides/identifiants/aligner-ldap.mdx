The institution's LDAP directory says whether an account is active and gives the grade,
employer and employment dates of staff. To benefit from it, each record must hold the
person's **uid**. The **LDAP alignment** page has two tabs: **Find missing** (link records
without a uid) and **Check existing ones** (carry over the directory's changes).

## Find missing: link records without a uid

1. Open **Find missing**.
2. Click **Run LDAP search**. Druid looks for each record without a uid in the directory, by
   **e-mail** then by **name**.
3. Go through the **Candidates**:
   - matches by e-mail are ticked;
   - matches by **name** are **unticked** by default (less certain): check the grade, title
     and date of birth shown, then tick.
4. In **Homonyms to decide**, choose the right account for each record, or **None (do not
   link)**.
5. Click **Link selection**.

If the uid is already held by another record of the same laboratory, Druid says so
(**duplicate of…**) and offers to **Merge** rather than create a duplicate.

At the bottom of the tab, the **LDAP uid without Directory record** list shows the people in
the directory who have no record in Druid.

## Check existing ones: carry over changes

1. Open **Check existing ones**.
2. Click **Re-run the LDAP sync**: Druid rereads the whole directory (several minutes, in the
   background) then compares it with the records.
3. Go through **To update**: for each record, the fields the directory has changed (status,
   employment, name…).
4. **Manually validated** records contradicted by the directory are marked **Validated —
   conflict** and **unticked**: check before overwriting them.
5. Tick or untick the rows, then click **Apply**.

The **Orphans** list shows the records whose uid no longer exists in the directory: probable
departure, to be checked on the record (status, employment end).

## What happens next

- The linked uids and updated fields are written to the shared directory.
- Only ticked records are changed; no record is created.
- Statuses are recalculated with the new data (see
  [Statuses of people](/en/donnees/statuts-des-personnes/)).

## Known limitations

- **People in the directory without a record** are not created automatically: their record
  must be created by hand.
- **Matching by name** can get namesakes wrong: only tick after checking.
- **Staff of other organisations** (CNRS, Inserm…) sometimes have a hosted account: their
  status follows their employer, not the state of the account (see
  [Statuses of people](/en/donnees/statuts-des-personnes/#1-the-employer)).
