Each institution has its own Druid instance, managed by a local **Druid team** (usually the
library or the research office). That is who you should contact, through your institution's
usual channel.

## Before writing

Check whether the help answers your question:

- wrong data: [Where to fix what](/en/guides/corriger/ou-corriger-quoi/);
- a missing publication: [A publication is missing](/en/guides/corriger/publication-manquante/);
- a section or a laboratory you cannot see:
  [First steps for your profile](/en/demarrer/premiers-pas/#requesting-additional-access);
- an unknown term: the [glossary](/en/demarrer/glossaire/).

The search at the top of this page covers the whole help. In Druid, the **Druid help** tab of
the assistant (button at the bottom right, depending on your instance) also answers from
these pages.

## What to include

For your request to be handled quickly, state:

| Request | What to include |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Your username, the laboratory or laboratories concerned, and what you need to be able to do (view, correct, administer). |
| **Wrong data** | The record concerned (name and laboratory, or uid), the wrong data and the correct value, with its source if possible. |
| **Dashboard** | The link to the view (**Copy link** button: it contains the structure, the tab, the period and the scope) and what surprises you. |
| **Publication** | The DOI or link of the publication, and the structure where it should (or should not) appear. |
| **Bug** | What you were doing, what happened, the exact error message, your browser. |

:::caution
Do not send screenshots containing personal data through an insecure channel: describe the
problem and give the link to the page.
:::

## For Druid administrators

A correction to be made **outside Druid** (on IdRef, ORCID, HAL, OpenAlex, in the LDAP
directory) is tracked in Druid: on the person's record, **Report a correction** creates a
task, visible in **People › To process › Tasks**. See
[Follow up “To process” tasks](/en/guides/personnes/taches-a-traiter/).

## Suggest an improvement to the help

A page is incomplete or unclear? Tell the Druid team, giving the page's address: the help
evolves with the application.
