Druid often knows information that is missing from researchers' IdRef records: their ORCID,
their IdHAL, their current affiliation. The **ABES export** produces a workbook listing these
enrichments, to send to ABES (or to be handled by the library) to update the records.

## Before the export

Review the records: the export relies on the last **Check existing ones** run for IdRef
([identifier alignment](/en/guides/identifiants/aligner-les-identifiants/) page). Druid flags
the records whose IdRef entry has not been reread.

## Steps

1. In **People**, filter the list: the export applies to the **filtered list** (for example
   one laboratory).
2. Open **Export › ABES export (IdRef)**.
3. Set the options:
   - **Preset**: limits the export to **Internal staff** (present and employed by the
     institution) whose record is validated;
   - **Validated records only**, **Exclude PhD students and adjuncts**;
   - **Propose 340 notes** (biographical note with the affiliation);
   - **“No record” sheet**: the people who have no IdRef record;
   - **Include rows already sent**.
4. Check the preview and the counters (records in scope, rows to send, conflicts, structures
   to create).
5. Download the **XLSX workbook**.
6. Once the file has been sent, click **Mark as sent**.

## The workbook

The sheet names are in French, the format expected by ABES:

| Sheet | Content |
|---|---|
| **enrichissements** | For each record, what needs to be added or corrected: identifiers (field 035), affiliation (field 510), note (field 340). |
| **conflits** | The cases where the IdRef record and Druid contradict each other: to check before sending anything. |
| **sans_notice** | People without an IdRef record, candidates for a creation. |
| **structures** | Structures that do not yet have an IdRef record. |

## What happens next

- **Mark as sent** dates the exported records: they will not be suggested again, except with
  **Include rows already sent**.
- The tasks of the **ABES batch** channel for these records are closed (see
  [Follow up “To process” tasks](/en/guides/personnes/taches-a-traiter/)).
- The records are updated by ABES or the library; Druid notices it at the next **Check
  existing ones** run.

## Known limitations

- **The export does not change IdRef**: it prepares the work.
- **A record that has not been reread** cannot be compared: run the IdRef check first.
- **Conflicts** need a human check: do not send them as they are.
