A **duplicate** is several records with the same uid, i.e. the same person. It is not always
a mistake: a person can belong to two laboratories. Druid asks you to decide, group by group.

## The three cases

The **Duplicates** page sorts the groups into three categories:

| Category | Situation | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Same lab** | Two records for the same laboratory, usually one row from the LDAP directory and one row created from the lab's website. | **Merge** |
| **Holding area** | One of the records has no laboratory (or a temporary one). | **Merge**: the record without a lab is absorbed by the lab's record. |
| **Several labs** | One record per laboratory. | **Qualify**: it is not necessarily a mistake. |

Groups that appeared since your last visit are labelled **new**. Groups already qualified are
filed under **Qualified multiple affiliations**.

## Merge two records

1. In the group, click **Merge**. The **Merge two records** wizard opens.
2. Check which record is **Kept**: Druid suggests the one with a laboratory, then the
   validated one, then the most complete. Click the other record to swap.
3. Go through the **Fields to decide**: for each field that differs, Druid pre-selects a
   value and says why (most recent validation, most recent LDAP update, union of both
   values, fills an empty field…). Change it if needed.
4. Click **Merge**.

The kept record is completed, the other one is deleted. The operation is **logged**: the
**Merge log**, at the bottom of the Duplicates page, lets you **Restore** it.

:::caution
If the two records do not have the same uid, Druid asks you to confirm that they really are
the same person. If in doubt, do not merge: open both records and check.
:::

## Qualify a multiple affiliation

1. In the group, click **Qualify**.
2. Choose the **Main row**: the record of the person's main laboratory.
3. Choose the type:
   - **Concurrent**: the person belongs to both laboratories at the same time. The other
     records become **secondary**;
   - **Successive**: the person moved to another laboratory. The other records become
     **former**; you can enter the end date of the former affiliation;
   - **To review**: undetermined case, remembered so that it does not come back at every
     review.
4. Confirm.

The group moves to **Qualified multiple affiliations**. You can **Requalify** it later.

## What happens next

- A merge changes the directory immediately: the absorbed record disappears from the lists,
  and a trace of the merge is added to the kept record.
- A qualification deletes nothing: it states, on each record, whether it is primary,
  secondary or former. The researcher record then shows all the person's memberships.
- Towards CRISalid, a person is exported only once, with their primary record.

## Known limitations

- **Only uid duplicates are detected**: two records of the same person with no shared uid
  (for example an external person entered twice under two spellings) are not spotted.
- **Restoring a merge puts the kept record back in its state before the merge**: changes
  made to that record in the meantime are lost.
- **The Refresh button recalculates the groups from the directory**, without a new
  synchronisation with the LDAP directory.
