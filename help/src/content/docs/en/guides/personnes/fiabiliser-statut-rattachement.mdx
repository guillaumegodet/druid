A person's status is calculated from the LDAP directory and the employment dates, which are
sometimes late or incomplete. When you know for sure that a person is present, has left or
is external, you **validate their record**: your information prevails over the calculation
for 18 months.

## Before validating: correct the data

Many status errors come from missing data. First check on the record:

- the **employing institution**: a CNRS or Inserm researcher must have their organisation as
  employer to be classified External;
- the **end dates**: a person who has left must have an **employment end** and a
  **membership end**. The **→ employment end** button copies one into the other;
- the **laboratory and team** in the Memberships card, and the primary affiliation.

Save. If the calculated status is then correct, a validation is only useful to protect the
record from the next directory updates.

## Validate a record

1. Open the person's record.
2. Click **Validate the record**.
3. Choose the **Validated status**: Internal, Leaving, Left or External.
4. Choose the **Scope**: the status, the affiliation, or both.
5. Enter the **Source**: where your information comes from (for example “Lab survey 2026,
   HR list”). The date and your identifier are added automatically.
6. Click **Validate**.

The **Validated** badge appears next to the status.

### Review or remove a validation

On a validated record, the **Validate the record** button becomes **Validated record**.
Click it:

- **Revalidate** resets the date to today, for example after checking an **Expired** record
  again;
- **Unvalidate** removes the validation: the calculated status applies again.

## Validate a list in one go

When a laboratory sends the list of its members, you can validate all the records at once.

1. In **People**, open **Synchronise › Import a validated list**.
2. Paste the list (columns **name**, **email**, **uid**, **status**, separated by commas,
   semicolons or tabs) or load a CSV file. A list of names only, without a header, also
   works.
3. Fill in the **Source (traceability)**, the **Validation date**, the **Validation scope**
   and the **Default status** (used for rows without a status).
4. Click **Analyse matching**. Druid finds each row by uid, then e-mail, then name, and
   shows three lists:
   - **Records that will be validated**;
   - **Homonyms**: several records match, nothing is applied;
   - **Not found**: no record matches.
5. Check the first list, then click **Validate N records**.

Then handle the homonyms and the rows not found by hand, record by record.

## What happens next

- The validation is written immediately to the directory, with its source, date and author.
- During the following synchronisations with the LDAP directory, validated records are not
  changed automatically; if the directory says otherwise, the badge becomes **Validated —
  conflict** and the row is flagged for a decision.
- Validated records become **members** of the structure for the dashboard (*Headcount*
  scope), at the next regeneration.
- After **18 months**, the badge changes to **Expired**: the validation still applies, but
  the record needs checking again.

## Known limitations

- **A past employment end and a past membership end prevail over the validation**: the
  person is then Left, even if validated as Internal (**Validated — conflict** badge).
- **The import matches by name as a last resort**: two people with the same name are
  classified as homonyms, never validated at random. Prefer lists with e-mail or uid.
- **A validation applies to one record**: a person with two records (two laboratories) must
  be validated on each.
- **The import is reserved** for technical administrators with access to the whole
  institution. Other profiles validate record by record, or send the list to
  [support](/en/guides/support/).
