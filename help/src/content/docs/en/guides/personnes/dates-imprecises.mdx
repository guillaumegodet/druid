The exact date of an arrival or a departure is not always known: a laboratory says “left in
2025”, an HR list gives a month. Druid accepts dates **to the year, the month or the day**,
so as not to invent a precision you do not have.

## The four dates of the record

| Card | Dates | What they describe |
|---|---|---|
| **Employment & contract** | **Employment start**, **Employment end** | The employment contract with the employer. |
| **Affiliations & history** | Membership start and end | Presence in the laboratory or the team. |

They do not always coincide: see
[Memberships and affiliation](/en/donnees/appartenances-rattachement/).

## Steps

1. Open the person's record.
2. In the date field, type what you know:

   | You know | Type |
   |---|---|
   | The year | `2025` |
   | The month | `2025-09` or `09/2025` |
   | The day | `2025-09-01` or `01/09/2025` (day/month/year) |

   For a full date, you can also use the calendar on the right of the field.
3. Leave the field: the date is put into Druid's format. An unreadable entry stays **red**
   and will not be saved.
4. For a departure, fill in the membership end and then click **→ employment end**: the same
   date is copied as the employment end.
5. Click **Save**.

## What happens next

- The date is saved **with its precision**: `2025` stays `2025`.
- The **status** is recalculated: a past employment end **and** a past membership end make
  the person **Left** (see
  [Statuses of people](/en/donnees/statuts-des-personnes/#1-certain-departure)).
- Towards CRISalid, an imprecise date is sent as the bound of its period: first day for a
  start, last day for an end.

## Known limitations

- **A period is only past once it is entirely over**: an end of `2026` only makes the person
  leave from 1 January 2027. If you know the departure took place in June, enter `2026-06`.
- **The date of birth** remains a full date.
- **The → employment end button** replaces an existing employment end: Druid warns you when
  it is different.
