Every week, Druid rebuilds the **career path** of the people of the Directory from three sources:

- the **affiliations of their publications**, in the CRISalid graph, OpenAlex and Scopus;
- the **positions declared in their ORCID profile**;
- their **Scopus author profile**.

It derives **signals** from them (probable departure, inconsistent status…) and **suggestions** of
actions on the identifiers.

## The « Career path » block of the record

- **The timeline**: one row per establishment, years across. Dots are the publications of the year,
  sized by their number. Bars are the positions declared in ORCID, dashed while ongoing. The grey band
  is the employment period entered in Druid.
- **The colours**: green for the institution and its site, blue for elsewhere, grey for national
  organisms (CNRS, Inserm…). Those organisms appear on the publications of every joint unit: they never
  count as a departure.
- **The table**: each establishment with its ORCID period, its presence in the Scopus profile, its
  period observed in the publications and their number. A click opens the list of those publications.
- **« Refresh »** recomputes the record right away, in about ten seconds, for instance after adding
  an identifier.

### Signals

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed departure | At least two sources agree (ORCID, publications, Scopus) |
| Declared departure | The last position at the institution is ended in ORCID |
| Observed departure | No publication affiliated to the institution for two years, several elsewhere |
| Current Scopus affiliation | Elsevier infers another affiliation from the latest publications. Weak on its own (multi-site units) |
| Inconsistent status | The record is ended, yet the person still publishes with the institution |
| Identifiers to check | The publications or the Scopus profile never mention the institution: namesake? |

The **« Report … as end of employment »** button fills the field of the record. Nothing is saved
before **Save** is clicked, and the status is never changed automatically.

In the **People** list, the **Career path** filter finds probable or confirmed departures,
inconsistent statuses and identifiers to check. An icon flags probable departures next to the name.
The same situations become tasks in **To process**, which close by themselves once the end date is
entered.

## Suggestions of the institution

The box suggests **at most five actions** on the person’s identifiers, the most useful first: a
missing identifier, then an error to fix, then a profile to complete.

| Suggestion | When | Where to act |
|---|---|---|
| Create an ORCID iD | no ORCID in the record | orcid.org |
| Make the ORCID profile public | empty or private profile | ORCID account settings |
| Add the position to ORCID | profile without any position, or without the one at the institution | ORCID › Employment |
| Close the position in ORCID | departure detected, position still ongoing in ORCID | ORCID › Employment |
| Link Scopus and ORCID | the ORCID profile does not list the Scopus Author ID | Elsevier’s Scopus-to-ORCID wizard |
| Create an IdHAL | no IdHAL (priority when HAL deposits exist) | HAL › My space › My IdHAL |
| Merge two IdHAL | two IdHAL detected | HAL support |
| Correct the Scopus profile | profile never affiliated to the institution, or mixed with namesakes | Elsevier Author Feedback Wizard |
| Merge two Scopus profiles | two profiles detected | Author Feedback Wizard |
| Merge the OpenAlex profiles | several OpenAlex profiles reviewed | openalex.org: « Claim » the profile then move the works to it |

- **The researcher** who opens their own record reads the same suggestions, worded for them.
- **Administrators** have three buttons:
  - **Prepare the email**: a template to review, copy or open in the mail client. Druid never sends
    anything itself.
  - **Create a task**: the follow-up in **To process**.
  - **Hide**: with a reason (already done, not relevant…). The suggestion does not come back, and the
    reason is kept in the task history.

A suggestion disappears by itself once the situation is settled, at the next computation of the
career path or of the alignments.
