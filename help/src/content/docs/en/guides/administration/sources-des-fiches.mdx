The civil status and the employment of a record (date of birth, grade, title, employment type, employer, employment
dates) keep the value of **each source**: LDAP directory, HR file, partner directory, lab website, lab file. The record
shows the value of the **most trusted source** that gives one. This page sets that order, field by field.

## Change the order of a field

1. Move the sources up or down with the arrows. A source that is **not ranked** (at the bottom of the card) comes after
   all the others; the **+** button adds it to the order.
2. Click **Preview**: Druid says how many records would change, with a few examples.
3. Click **Save and recompute**: the records concerned are recomputed at once.

A **starting profile** fills the order of every field at once: « University with an LDAP directory » (the default
order) or « Without LDAP » (the HR export first). Each field still has to be previewed and saved.

**Export (JSON)** downloads the orders in force, for example to reuse them on another instance.

## What cannot be set

- **A correction typed in a record always wins**, whatever the order: it is not part of it.
- The rules of each field (precision of the dates, case of the grades) are the same everywhere.

## What happens next

The new order applies at once to the records and to every later import. It is written to the change log. Druid
updates do not modify it.
