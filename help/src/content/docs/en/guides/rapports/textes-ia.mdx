Two texts of a report can be written by AI (ILAAS service): a **summary** and an **analysis by
major theme**. **All figures are computed by Druid**; the AI groups existing topics and writes.
Each text can be edited and must be reviewed.

## The two texts

| Text | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **AI analysis by major theme** | The topics of the corpus grouped into major themes; for each one, the number of publications, the trend, notable people and publications, and a synthesis. Small themes only get their figures. |
| **AI summary** | A summary, key points and leads (for cooperation in a collaboration report, for action otherwise), written from the key figures and the analysis by theme of the report. |

## Steps

1. Add an **AI text** block (the collaboration, funding and journals templates already have
   some) and click it to open its settings.
2. Choose the text: **AI summary** or **AI analysis by major theme**.
3. Click **Generate**. The analysis by theme takes one to two minutes (« Writing… » shows the
   progress). Generate it **before** the summary, which relies on it.
4. Read the text in the preview; correct it directly in its settings.
5. Tick **I have reviewed this text (printed in the PDF)**.

## What happens next

The text is saved with the report; it no longer changes until you click **Regenerate**. In the
PDF, it is framed with the note « Text generated by AI (ILAAS, model) — not reviewed yet » or
« — reviewed by … on … ».

## Known limitations

- **An AI text not generated yet is not printed**: the preview says so (« not generated yet »).
- **Regenerate replaces your corrections**: review again after each regeneration.
- **The AI can misinterpret**: Druid drops the themes, names and titles it makes up, but not a
  misreading. Check every statement against the charts of the report.
- **Trends are only commented** from 10 publications, impact comparisons from 5 values.
- **The service can be slow or unavailable**: Druid retries automatically; if it still fails,
  try again later.
- **The data sent** to ILAAS are figures, topic labels, publication titles and author names.
