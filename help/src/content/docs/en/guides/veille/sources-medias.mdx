The media watch collects every night the mentions of the institution, its laboratories and
its researchers in open sources. The **Media sources** page lets you choose these sources and
add by hand a mention that the collection did not find.

## View the sources monitored

The **Harvested sources** list shows, for each source, its **Type**, its **Scope** and its
**Health**:

| Health | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Last collection successful (date shown). |
| **Watch** | Several collections in a row without any article. |
| **Down** | The source no longer responds. |
| **Never collected** | Source added, not yet through a collection. |

Sources marked **built-in** come with Druid and cannot be deleted.

## Add an RSS feed

1. In **Add an RSS feed**, paste the **Feed URL (RSS/Atom)** and give it a **Name** and a
   **Media type**.
2. Choose the **Scope**:
   - **Institution**: a feed of the institution or the lab itself, all its articles are
     kept;
   - **Generic (keyword-filtered)**: a general-interest outlet, only the articles that
     mention a laboratory or the institution are kept.
3. For a laboratory feed, enter the **Labs concerned** (acronyms separated by commas).
4. Click **Test and add**: Druid checks that the feed responds before adding it.

**Delete this feed** removes a source you added.

## Add a mention by hand

For an article that the collection did not find (digitised print press, site without a
feed):

1. In **Add a news item from a URL**, paste the **Article URL**.
2. Fill in the **Title**, the **Media outlet**, the **Publication date**, an optional
   **Excerpt** and the **Labs concerned** (or tick **Institution**).
3. Click **Publish**.

The mention is published immediately in the **Mentions** tab of the Watch, without going
through validation.

## What happens next

New sources are collected from the following night. Detected mentions arrive in the **To
review** queue of the Watch tab (see
[Follow new publications](/en/guides/veille/nouvelles-publications/#follow-media-presence)).

## Known limitations

- **Only open sources** (public feeds) can be followed: no subscription press.
- **A general-interest outlet** produces false positives when a laboratory's name is also a
  common word: validation discards them.
- **A feed without articles for a long time** becomes **Watch** without necessarily being
  down (a site with little activity).
