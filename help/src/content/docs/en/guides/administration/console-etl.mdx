The **ETL console** configures, for each structure, how its publications are harvested, and
starts the calculation of its dashboard. It is where you create the dashboard of a new
laboratory, correct its scope or update it.

## Open a structure

- From **Administration › ETL console**, choose the structure;
- or, from its dashboard, click **Configure**.

The header shows the state of the last generation (**Finished on…**, **Failed on…**,
**Running since…**) and the date of the dashboard.

## Configure

The configuration is organised in four sections:

| Section | Main settings |
|---|---|
| **1. Structure identity** | Acronym, full name, start and end years, term for internal groupings (“team”), research FTE (enables the output ratios). |
| **2. Data sources** | OpenAlex institution identifier(s), BSO affiliation markers (acronym, name, RNSR…), URL of the BSO dump, HAL collection and start year, identifier of the structure in the CRISalid graph, OpenAlex contact e-mail. |
| **3. Grist and affiliation checks** | Acronym of the structure in the directory (LABO field), positive and negative affiliation keywords, signature template of the charter. |
| **4. Sub-structures and display** | Laboratories of a composite institution, hidden tabs of the dashboard, restricting the corpus to the publications of the headcount. |

- **Save the configuration** is enough for hidden tabs and identity: the dashboard takes them
  into account without a regeneration.
- **Save and regenerate the data** reruns the harvesting and the calculation.

## Regenerate a dashboard

1. Click **Save and regenerate the data**.
2. Follow the progress and the **Log of the last generation**. Allow from a few minutes to
   an hour depending on the size of the structure.
3. Once finished, **View the dashboard**.

At each generation, the **headcount** (the structure's validated records in the directory,
excluding people who are leaving), the journal quartiles and their access status are updated
automatically.

## Create the dashboard of a new structure

1. Click **➕ New structure**, fill in the acronym and the name, then **Create the
   structure**.
2. Complete at least section 2 (OpenAlex identifier, BSO markers, HAL collection) and the
   acronym in section 3.
3. **Save and regenerate the data**.

## Maintenance

- **Staff (Grist directory)**: how many members were kept (validated, not validated
  ignored, people leaving ignored). **Resynchronise from Grist** updates the list without
  regenerating.
- **OpenAlex affiliation checks**: detects publications with a suspicious affiliation and
  follows up their correction (see
  [An affiliation is wrong](/en/guides/corriger/affiliation-fausse/)).

## What happens next

The generation produces the dashboard data. Users see the new figures as soon as the
generation ends, by reloading the page.

## Known limitations

- **One generation at a time** for the whole instance: a request waits for the current
  generation to finish.
- **OpenAlex quotas** limit the number of full regenerations per day.
- **Members that are not validated are ignored**: a headcount that is too low often comes
  from records not validated in the directory.
- **A wrong OpenAlex identifier** or BSO markers that are too broad bring in publications
  from other structures: check the Sources tab and the publication list after each change.
