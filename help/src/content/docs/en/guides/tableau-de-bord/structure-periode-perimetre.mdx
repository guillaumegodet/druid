Three settings at the top of the dashboard determine **all** the data shown in the tabs: the
structure, the period and the scope.

## Steps

### 1. The structure or group

In the **Structure or group** selector, choose:

- a **structure**: a laboratory, a faculty, an institution;
- or a **group** of researchers (a council, a project), if you have access to it.

From a structure's record, the **Dashboard** button opens that structure's dashboard
directly.

### 2. The period

Set the years with **From** … **to** …. All indicators, charts and lists are limited to the
publications of those years.

### 3. The scope

| Scope | Publications kept |
|---|---|
| **Affiliation** | All publications signed with the structure's affiliation. |
| **Headcount** | Only those with at least one author recognised among the structure's validated members. |

Hovering over the selector shows how many publications the *Headcount* scope leaves out. The
default scope depends on the structure's configuration.

See [Publications and attribution](/en/donnees/publications-attribution/#the-two-scopes-of-the-dashboard)
to choose the right scope.

### 4. Browse the families of analyses

The analyses are grouped in **seven families** (first row of buttons); the open family shows its
views on a second row. The **Overview** ends with an **Explore the analyses** grid describing each
family and opening its views directly.

| Family | Views | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | — | Key figures, trends, publication types, open access, keywords. |
| **Publications** | Books · Journals · Publication list · Sources | Books and chapters; journals and quartiles; all publications, filterable and exportable; where they come from (CRISalid, BSO, OpenAlex, HAL). |
| **Impact** | Impact and citations · Benchmark | FWCI, top 1% / 10%, by team and by researcher; comparison with comparable universities. |
| **Collaborations** | Partnerships · Network | National and international collaborations, partner countries and institutions; co-authorship network. |
| **People** | Directory · Researchers · Teams · PhD students | Today's directory (presence, employers, grades, gender balance, identifiers); publication rate per FTE and age pyramid; output by team; PhD students. |
| **Steering** | Strategic axes · Funding · APC monitoring · Signature charter | Axes configured for the structure; funders cited; publication fees; compliance of affiliations. |
| **Watch** | — | New publications, media mentions, newsletter. |

A view can be hidden for a structure by the technical team; a family without any view disappears.
Links shared with the former address of a tab (`?tab=…`) still open the right view.

## Share the view

**Copy link** copies the address of the view on display: structure, tab, period and scope.
Whoever opens it gets exactly the same settings (if they have access to the structure).

## What happens next

Nothing is changed: these settings only change the display. They are kept in the page
address, which you can bookmark.

## Known limitations

- **The data date from the last generation** of the dashboard, not from today (see
  [Where the data come from](/en/donnees/sources-des-donnees/)).
- **The Headcount scope is not available** if the structure's members are not recognised in
  the corpus: the dashboard then stays on *Affiliation*.
- **A structure without data** shows a message: its dashboard has not been generated yet.
  See [support](/en/guides/support/).
