Each cell of the alignment page sums up a person's situation for one source. Clicking the
badge opens the **details**: the candidates, their evidence and the possible actions.

## Read a candidate

For each candidate, the details show:

- its **identifier**, with a link to the profile (open it if in doubt);
- its **level**: **strong**, **medium** or **weak**;
- its **evidence**: shared identifier, affiliation found, shared publications, name forms;
- what is **Already in the Directory** for this person.

Before validating a medium or weak candidate, open the profile: same topics, same
co-authors, same institution?

## Decide case by case

| Badge | Action |
|---|---|
| **strong candidate** | Tick the box in the badge. |
| **candidates to check** | Open the details, check the profile, tick the right candidate. |
| **Ambiguous — decide** | Choose the right candidate among those suggested, or **Ignore (write nothing)**. |
| **conflict** | The record already has another identifier. Druid writes nothing: read the reason, then correct the record by hand if the existing identifier is wrong. |
| **replaced entry** (IdRef) | ABES has merged or deleted the authority record. **Update** replaces the old PPN with the new one. If Druid reports a **name mismatch**, check on IdRef first. |
| **Name mismatch** (IdRef) | The name of the authority record differs from the directory's: confirm (marriage, name in use) or detach the record. |
| **filled in** | Nothing to do. |

The ticked choices are written in one go with **Apply (N)**, at the bottom of the page.

## Discard a wrong candidate

If a candidate is a namesake, click **Wrong candidate**. The (person, identifier) pair is
blacklisted: it will **never be suggested again**, even after a new search.

## Report a mixed identity

Sometimes a profile (IdRef, HAL, OpenAlex, Scopus) **mixes up several people**: it holds the
works of the person and those of a namesake. Neither validate it (you would harvest someone
else's works) nor reject it (you would lose theirs).

1. Click **Mixed identity**.
2. The profile leaves the suggestions; a report is opened in the review table.
3. Have the profile corrected **at the source**: IdRef by the library, HAL by the author or
   the laboratory's HAL contact, OpenAlex by the author or OpenAlex support. Note the request
   in the review table.

Open reports are listed in the **Mixed identities — to untangle at the source** section of
the page. Druid also flags **suspicions** (“suspected mixed identity” badge): it is a clue,
never a decision.

## What happens next

- **Apply** writes the identifiers to the directory. For IdRef, ORCID and HAL, validating a
  candidate discards the person's other candidates; for OpenAlex, each profile is decided
  separately (a person can have several).
- **Wrong candidate** and **Mixed identity** are saved immediately.
- **Ignore** writes nothing: the record will come back at the next run.

## Known limitations

- **An empty ORCID profile** (“empty record”) cannot be confirmed: ask the person to make
  their information public, or to send you their ORCID.
- **Compound names and name changes** give lower scores: a good candidate can appear as
  “to check”.
- **An error in the source** (an IdRef record mixing two people, a fragmented OpenAlex
  profile) cannot be fixed in Druid.
