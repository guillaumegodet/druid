The **Publication list** tab shows, one by one, the publications behind the dashboard's
figures. It is the tool for checking an indicator, finding a publication or preparing a
list.

## Steps

1. First set the structure, the period and the scope at the top of the dashboard (see
   [Choose a structure, a period, a scope](/en/guides/tableau-de-bord/structure-periode-perimetre/)).
2. Open the **Publication list** tab. The number of publications in the period is shown.
3. Type a word of the **title**, a **journal** name or a **DOI** in the search box.
4. Click **Show filters** and combine the criteria you need:

| Family | Filters |
|---|---|
| Publication | Year, Document type, Language, Domain, Subfield |
| Authors | Team, Lab, Employment type, PhD student involved |
| Journal | Journal (exact name), SJR quartile |
| Access | Open access, APC paid, National licence, the institution's access to the journal |
| Impact | Top 10% citations, Top 1% citations |
| Collaboration | Collaboration, International collaboration, Partner country |
| Quality | Compliant with the charter, Sources (provenance), Strategic axis |

5. Active filters appear as tags: **Remove this filter** removes one, **Clear all** removes
   them all.
6. Go through the results page by page (**Previous**, **Next**).

:::tip
In the other tabs, some charts link to the list already filtered: it is the quickest way to
see which publications make up a bar or a slice.
:::

## Export the list

**Export as CSV (filtered results)** downloads the publications on display, with your
filters, as a file you can open in a spreadsheet.

## What happens next

Nothing is changed: filters only change the display.

## Known limitations

- **Filters depend on the data available**: a publication without a DOI often has an unknown
  open-access status, a publication outside a journal has no quartile.
- **The Team and Employment type filters** only apply to authors recognised among the
  structure's members.
- **The Journal filter** expects the exact name of the journal; for an approximate search,
  use the search box.
